About

Hey, I'm Ankush. I write about engineering, scaling teams, startups and more recently about LLM and agentic infrastructure.

I've enjoyed a fair share of ambiguity and building things from scratch. I personally tried and consulted clients doing a lot of -1 to 0 journeys building various agentic and AI infrastructure ideas around QA agents, video editing & multi-modal workflows, enterprise AI governance, marketing agents, etc. Recently I'm working on a seed-stage GPU neocloud and enterprise AI startup named AION, deep diving into the AI infrastructure landscape. I was also an early engineering lead at Koinex, a prominent crypto exchange out of India, in 2018. A long career at Google, where I shipped on the intersection of ML & IoT, and a brief one at PayPal taught me what scale looks like, and why I keep going back to zero.

I love open source too. I built ptop, a terminal task manager for macOS and Linux, did a couple of Google Summer of Code stints (syslog-ng, GNU Mailman), and once talked about Python and HCI at PyCon India.

If you are building something cool and like my work, let's share notes. Drop me a mail — I usually reply within a day.
